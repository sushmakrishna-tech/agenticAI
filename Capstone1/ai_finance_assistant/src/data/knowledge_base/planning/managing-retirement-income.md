Defined contribution plans carry no guarantee that your balance will last, which makes you responsible for saving enough and making it last. Longevity risk is the risk of outliving your assets, and a pension's lifetime annuity protects against it, while 401(k) plans typically offer no annuity option. Allocation usually changes as your time horizon shortens and you near a goal.
