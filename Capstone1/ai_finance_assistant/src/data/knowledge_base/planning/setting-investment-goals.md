Common goals include retirement, college, and a house down payment. The first step is estimating the true cost of each goal, then adjusting it to fit your resources, risk comfort, and time frame. Revisit goals regularly. Separate accounts for major goals can make progress easier to track, and short-term goals like a vacation usually call for different products than college or retirement.
