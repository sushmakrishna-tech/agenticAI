A defined benefit plan, or pension, promises a specified monthly retirement benefit, and the employer bears the investment risk. A defined contribution plan, such as a 401(k), promises no set payment. You or your employer contribute to your account, you bear the investment risk, and your balance depends on contributions plus or minus investment results.
