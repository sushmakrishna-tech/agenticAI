A 529 plan, legally a qualified tuition plan, is a tax-advantaged way to save for certain education costs. The two types are prepaid tuition plans and education savings plans. All 50 states and the District of Columbia sponsor at least one type. The SEC does not regulate or oversee 529 plans.
