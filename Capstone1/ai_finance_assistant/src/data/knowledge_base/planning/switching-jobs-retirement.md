A job change raises questions about paycheck gaps and insurance coverage. For a 401(k) or 403(b), the options are cashing out, rolling into a new employer's plan if it accepts transfers, rolling into an IRA, or possibly leaving the money where it is. Cashing out means owing taxes and possibly penalties if you are under age 59½.
