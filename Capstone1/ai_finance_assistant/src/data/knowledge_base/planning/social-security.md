Social Security is funded by payroll taxes and also supports disabled workers and families after a spouse or parent dies. Your monthly retirement benefit depends on your earnings and when you retire, and people who retire earlier typically receive lower payments. A my Social Security account shows statements, earnings, and benefit estimates.
