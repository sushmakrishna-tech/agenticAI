When starting a job, check whether your employer offers a plan such as a 401(k) or 403(b). Some employers enroll you automatically, but you often must enroll yourself. Compare fund fees, since small cost differences add up over time.

## Employer match
Employers may match contributions up to a certain level. If an employer adds 50 cents per dollar you save, that is an immediate 50% return, which Investor.gov describes as a guaranteed return no other investment offers.
