Retirees are often targeted by fraud. Protective habits include researching the company and salesperson independently, being wary of unsolicited offers, refusing to be rushed, and watching for salespeople who play on your fears. Be suspicious if you cannot retrieve your principal or cash out profits. A product that sounds too good to be true is a warning sign.
