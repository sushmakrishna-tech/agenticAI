An annuity is a contract with an insurance company, often considered for retirement or for turning assets into income. Money in it grows tax-deferred. Annuities may be immediate or deferred, and they come in fixed, variable, and indexed types with different risks and rewards.

## Costs and access
Annuities can be complex and costly, and added features called riders cost extra. Variable annuities can have surrender periods of eight years or more, with penalties for cashing out early.
