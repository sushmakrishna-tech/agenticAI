Banks, savings associations, and credit unions offer savings and checking accounts, money market deposit accounts, and CDs. Bank deposits are protected by the FDIC and credit union deposits by the NCUA, up to a limit. These products are typically lower risk and easy to access, but usually pay lower interest than other investment options.
