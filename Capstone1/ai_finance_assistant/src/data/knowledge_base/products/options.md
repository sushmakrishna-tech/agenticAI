An option is a derivative giving the buyer the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell an asset at a fixed price by a set date. The seller takes on the obligation if the buyer exercises that right. A call gives the right to buy, and a put gives the right to sell.

## Risks
Options offer leverage, which can magnify results but carries the risk of significant losses. Trading them requires specific approval from your brokerage firm.
