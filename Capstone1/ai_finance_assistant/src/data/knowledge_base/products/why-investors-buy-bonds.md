Buying a bond is lending to a government, municipality, or corporation, which promises interest and repayment of principal when the bond matures. Investors buy bonds for predictable income, often paid on a regular schedule such as every six months. Holding to maturity returns the full principal, which helps preserve capital, and bonds can help offset more volatile stock holdings.
