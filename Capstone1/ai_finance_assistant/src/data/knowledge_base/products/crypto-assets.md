A crypto asset is issued or transferred using distributed ledger or blockchain technology. Types include bitcoin, ether, stablecoins, and non-fungible tokens (NFTs). Investors can also get exposure through exchange-traded products or companies involved in crypto.

## Risks
Crypto assets can be exceptionally risky and volatile, and they often lack the regulatory protections of stocks and bonds. Fraud and scams are common. FINRA warns against investing based on social media or fear of missing out. It also marks this page as having updates pending, so rules may have changed.
