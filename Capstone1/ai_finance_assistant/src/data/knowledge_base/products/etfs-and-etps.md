Exchange-traded products (ETPs) are listed on an exchange and trade throughout the day like a stock. They track underlying assets or benchmarks. The most common type is the exchange-traded fund (ETF), a pooled investment that typically holds a basket of stocks, bonds, or other assets. Some ETPs offer cost-effective diversification, but others do not.

## ETNs are different
Exchange-traded notes (ETNs) hold no assets. They are debt securities issued by a bank or other financial institution, similar to corporate bonds. All ETPs have fees, described in the prospectus.
