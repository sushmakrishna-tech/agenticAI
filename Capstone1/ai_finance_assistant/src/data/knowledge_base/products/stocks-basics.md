Stocks, also called equities, represent an ownership stake in a company, and your return depends on how that company fares. FINRA notes that new investors may want to consider stock funds rather than picking individual stocks as a cost-effective way to diversify.

## Risks
Stocks have historically outperformed bonds over the long term, but their prices fluctuate and can fall dramatically. That volatility makes stocks risky for short-term goals.
