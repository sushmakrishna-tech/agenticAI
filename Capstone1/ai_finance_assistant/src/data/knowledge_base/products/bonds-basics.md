A bond is a debt security: the issuer borrows money and agrees to pay interest. At maturity, it pays the final interest payment and the face value, also called par value. Bonds that mature in one to three years are generally short-term, and those maturing in four to 10 years are intermediate-term.

## Prices and interest rates
Bonds can help diversify a portfolio and tend to be less volatile than stocks, though their prices still fluctuate. When interest rates rise, bond prices tend to fall, and vice versa. U.S. Treasury securities carry relatively lower risk.
