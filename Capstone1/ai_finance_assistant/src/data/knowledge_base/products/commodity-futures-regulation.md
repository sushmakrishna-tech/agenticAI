The SEC does not regulate commodity futures. The Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) regulates futures trading, and anyone trading futures with the public or advising on them must be registered with the National Futures Association. The CFTC warns that high-yield offers in futures, options, or forex are common areas of fraud.
