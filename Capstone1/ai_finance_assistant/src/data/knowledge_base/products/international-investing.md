People invest internationally for diversification across foreign companies and markets and for growth potential, particularly in emerging markets. Special risks include less or different company information, which may not be in English, and higher costs. U.S. investors who deal directly with an unregistered foreign broker may not have the same protections as with an SEC-registered one.
