Commodities are basic goods such as metals, oil, grains, and livestock, and they can add diversification. Investors can hold a physical good, trade futures contracts, or use mutual funds and exchange-traded products. A futures contract is a binding agreement to buy or sell at a future date for a fixed price and quantity. Leverage can amplify gains and losses, and leveraged or inverse products add further risk.
