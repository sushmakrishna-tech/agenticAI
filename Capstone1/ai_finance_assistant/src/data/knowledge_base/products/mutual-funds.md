A mutual fund pools money from many investors to buy securities toward a stated objective such as growth or income. Investors can buy and redeem shares daily based on net asset value (NAV). Some funds focus on one product, industry, or region, while others seek to replicate a market index. Funds can offer cost-effective diversification and professional management.

## Costs
All mutual funds have fees and expenses, and different share classes of the same fund can cost different amounts. A fund's prospectus describes its objectives, strategies, and costs, and FINRA's Fund Analyzer compares them.
