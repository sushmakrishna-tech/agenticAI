People buy stocks for capital appreciation, dividends, and the ability to vote their shares. Common stock carries voting rights and dividends. Preferred stockholders usually cannot vote, but they receive dividends before common stockholders and have priority if a bankrupt company's assets are liquidated.

## Growth stocks
Growth stocks have earnings rising faster than the market average. They rarely pay dividends, so investors buy them hoping for price gains.
