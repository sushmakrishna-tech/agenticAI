A CD holds a fixed amount for a fixed period, such as six months or five years, in exchange for interest. CDs from federally insured banks are insured up to $250,000, and that limit covers all accounts in your name at the same bank rather than each CD.

## What to check
The disclosure should show the rate, whether it is fixed or variable, the maturity date, and early withdrawal penalties. The main risk is inflation growing faster than your money.
