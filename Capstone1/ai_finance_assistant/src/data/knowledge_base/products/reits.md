A REIT is a company that owns and typically operates income-producing real estate or related assets, such as apartments, offices, warehouses, or mortgages. It lets individuals earn a share of real estate income without buying property themselves. Publicly traded REITs trade on a stock exchange, while non-traded REITs may be registered with the SEC without being exchange listed. That distinction affects the benefits and risks.
