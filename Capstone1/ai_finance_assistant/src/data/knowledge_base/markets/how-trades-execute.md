Placing a trade online does not connect you directly to the market. Your order goes to your broker, who decides which market to send it to. This is usually quick but takes time, and prices can move, especially in fast markets. You may not receive the price you saw, because quotes apply only to a specific number of shares. SEC rules do not require execution within a set time, but firms advertising their speed must not exaggerate it.
