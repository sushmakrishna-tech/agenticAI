A public company is generally one with public reporting obligations. Companies take on those obligations by selling securities in a public offering such as an IPO, by growing their investor base to a certain size, or by registering voluntarily. A private company can also become subject to them by merging with a public shell company, called a reverse merger. Investor.gov urges caution with those companies.
