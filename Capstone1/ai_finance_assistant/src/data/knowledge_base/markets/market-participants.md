The SEC regulates many market participants. Broker-dealers handle trades between buyers and sellers for a fee and may trade from their own inventory. Clearing corporations compare and clear transactions and prepare them for settlement. Depositories hold securities certificates and maintain ownership records. Credit rating agencies give graded opinions on the creditworthiness of a company or security.
