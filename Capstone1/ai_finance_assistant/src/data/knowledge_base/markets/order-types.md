A market order buys or sells immediately. It guarantees execution but not the price, and the last traded price may not be the price you get. A limit order executes only at a set price or better, so a buy limit at $10 fills only at $10 or lower.

## Stop orders
A stop order, or stop-loss order, becomes a market order once the stock reaches a set stop price. A sell stop sits below the current price and is generally used to limit a loss or protect a profit on stock you own.
