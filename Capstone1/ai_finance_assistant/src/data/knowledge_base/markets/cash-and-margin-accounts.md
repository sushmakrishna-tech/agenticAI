In a cash account, you pay the full amount for securities. In a margin account, your brokerage firm lends you money using your portfolio as collateral, and you pay interest on the loan.

## Margin calls
If your securities fall in value, the firm can require more cash or securities immediately. It can also sell your securities without notifying you first, and it chooses which ones to sell. It may sell even if it gave you a deadline to cover the shortfall.
