Buying or selling securities typically requires a brokerage or other investment account. Discount firms may offer low- or zero-commission trading with limited services, while full-service firms may offer an investment professional, research, and planning. The firm remains responsible for routing, execution, and settlement even when you trade through an app. FINRA BrokerCheck shows the registration and background of firms and professionals. Before trading, understand the fees, order types, and tax implications.
