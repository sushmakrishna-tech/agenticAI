A Roth IRA generally follows the same rules as a traditional IRA, with some key differences. Contributions are not deductible, but qualified distributions are tax-free. You can keep contributing after age 70½ and can leave money in the account for as long as you live. The account must be designated as a Roth IRA when it is set up.

## Contribution limits
The same combined annual limit applies across all of your Roth and traditional IRAs. Your allowed Roth contribution may also be reduced based on your filing status and income.
