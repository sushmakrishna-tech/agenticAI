You generally must start withdrawals from IRAs and retirement plans at age 73. Roth IRAs and designated Roth accounts need no withdrawals while the owner is alive, though beneficiaries are subject to the rules. The amount is the prior year-end balance divided by an IRS life expectancy factor, usually from the Uniform Lifetime Table. The first IRA withdrawal is due by April 1 of the year after you reach 73.
