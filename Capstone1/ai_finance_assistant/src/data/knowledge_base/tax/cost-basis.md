Basis is generally what you paid for an asset, and it is used to figure gain or loss when you sell. For stocks and bonds, it is the purchase price plus costs such as commissions and transfer fees. Gifts and inheritances follow different rules. Certain events during ownership can raise or lower it, resulting in an adjusted basis.
