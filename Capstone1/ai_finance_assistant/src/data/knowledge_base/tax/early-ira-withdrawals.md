Withdrawing from a traditional or Roth IRA before age 59½ generally triggers a 10% additional tax on the part included in income, on top of regular income tax.

## Exceptions
Exceptions include rollovers, total and permanent disability, terminal illness, certain medical costs, qualified higher education expenses, up to $10,000 for a first home purchase, and up to $5,000 for a birth or adoption.
