Some exceptions to the 10% early withdrawal tax apply to both plans and IRAs: birth or adoption up to $5,000 per child, disaster recovery up to $22,000, and one yearly emergency distribution up to $1,000. Others differ. Qualified higher education expenses are an exception for IRAs but not for 401(k)-type plans. Form 5329 is used to report distributions subject to the tax.
