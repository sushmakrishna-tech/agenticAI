Distributions from qualified plans such as a 401(k) or 403(b) before age 59½ generally face a 10% additional tax on the portion included in income. Eligible 457 plans are generally not subject to it, except for amounts rolled in from qualified plans.

## Exceptions
Exceptions include rollovers, disability, terminal illness, payments after death, and leaving your employer after reaching age 55. Exceptions for plans and IRAs differ.
