These tax year 2026 amounts generally apply to returns filed in 2027.

## Standard deduction
The 2026 standard deduction is $16,100 for single filers and married people filing separately, $32,200 for married couples filing jointly, and $24,150 for heads of household.

## Marginal tax rates
For 2026, rates for single filers are 10% on income up to $12,400, 12% over $12,400, 22% over $50,400, 24% over $105,700, 32% over $201,775, 35% over $256,225, and 37% over $640,600. For married couples filing jointly, the thresholds are 12% over $24,800, 22% over $100,800, 24% over $211,400, 32% over $403,550, 35% over $512,450, and 37% over $768,700. A marginal rate applies only to the income within that bracket, not to all of your income.
