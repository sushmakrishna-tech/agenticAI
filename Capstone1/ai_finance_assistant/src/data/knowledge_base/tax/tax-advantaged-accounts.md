These accounts offer tax benefits to encourage saving for specific purposes. Benefits can include deductions, tax-deferred growth, and tax-free withdrawals. Examples include retirement accounts such as 401(k)s and IRAs, 529 plans for education, HSAs for medical costs, ABLE accounts for disability expenses, and Trump Accounts for minors.
