---
title: "Retirement Plan Rollovers"
category: tax
source_name: "IRS Topic 413"
source_url: https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc413

---
A rollover moves money from one eligible retirement plan to another within 60 days. It is reportable but not taxable, unless it goes from another type of account into a Roth IRA or designated Roth account. Any taxable amount you do not roll over counts as income that year. Required minimum distributions and hardship distributions are among those that cannot be rolled over.
