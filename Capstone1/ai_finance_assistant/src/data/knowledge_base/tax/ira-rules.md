You can set up an IRA with a bank, insurance company, or other financial institution, and IRAs cannot be owned jointly. To contribute, you or your spouse on a joint return must have taxable compensation, such as wages, tips, or self-employment income. Rental income, interest, dividends, and pensions do not count. There is no age limit. You may also qualify for a tax credit for retirement savings contributions.
