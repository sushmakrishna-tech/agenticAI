These are tax-deferred retirement plans from certain employers. Public schools, certain non-profits, and churches may offer 403(b) plans, and state and local governments and certain non-profits may offer 457(b) plans. Pretax contributions and earnings are not taxed until withdrawn. They typically offer mutual funds and annuities.
