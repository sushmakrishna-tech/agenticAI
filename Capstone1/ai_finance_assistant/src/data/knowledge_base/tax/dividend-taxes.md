All taxable dividends are ordinary dividends, and qualified dividends are the ones taxed at lower capital gain rates. The payer identifies them on Form 1099-DIV. Mutual funds, ETFs, and REITs also pay dividends and may pay capital gain distributions, which are always reported as long-term gains.

## Return of capital
A return of capital is not a dividend. It returns part of your investment and reduces your cost basis in the stock.
