Pretax 401(k) deferrals are not subject to income tax withholding when made, but they are still subject to Social Security and Medicare taxes. Some plans also allow after-tax designated Roth contributions. Your plan sets when you can access the money, and many plans allow hardship withdrawals for immediate and heavy financial needs.
