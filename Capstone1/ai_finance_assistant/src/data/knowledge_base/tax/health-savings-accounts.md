An HSA is a tax-advantaged account for qualified medical expenses. It can serve as a spending account for current costs, savings for unexpected costs, and, where the HSA allows investing in funds, a way to grow money for future medical expenses, including in retirement. The SEC does not regulate or oversee HSAs.
