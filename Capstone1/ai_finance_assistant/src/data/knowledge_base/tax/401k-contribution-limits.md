Two annual limits apply to a 401(k): one on what an employee defers from salary, and an overall limit on all contributions to the account, including employer contributions. For 2026, the employee deferral limit for traditional and safe harbor 401(k) plans is $24,500. For a SIMPLE 401(k), it is $17,000. A plan's own terms may set a lower limit.

## Catch-up contributions
If the plan allows them, participants who are age 50 or older by the end of the calendar year can contribute more. For 2026, the catch-up amount is $8,000 for traditional and safe harbor plans and $4,000 for SIMPLE 401(k) plans. Employees aged 60 to 63 get a higher catch-up limit: $11,250 for traditional and safe harbor plans and $5,250 for SIMPLE plans.

## More than one plan
Your deferrals across all plans you participate in are generally added together when checking the limit. These amounts are adjusted for cost of living over time.
