Most interest is taxable in the year it becomes available to you, including from bank accounts, CDs, money market accounts, and corporate bonds. You must report all taxable and tax-exempt interest even without a Form 1099-INT, which payers generally send for $10 or more.

## Treasuries and savings bonds
Interest from Treasury bills, notes, and bonds is subject to federal income tax but exempt from state and local income taxes. Interest on Series EE and I savings bonds is generally not included until they mature or are redeemed, unless you elect to report it yearly.
