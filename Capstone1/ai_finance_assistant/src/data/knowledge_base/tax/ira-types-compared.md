With a traditional IRA, earnings are not taxed until retirement, when withdrawals are taxed as income. With a Roth IRA, contributions are made with after-tax dollars, and earnings and withdrawals are generally tax-free. A SEP IRA lets small businesses or self-employed people contribute to a traditional IRA in an employee's name. A SIMPLE IRA serves small businesses with no other plan and allows employer and employee contributions at lower limits than a 401(k).
