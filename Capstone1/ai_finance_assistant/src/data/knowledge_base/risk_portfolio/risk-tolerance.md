The right products depend on when you need the money, your goals, and your comfort with possible losses. Someone with 35 years until retirement may consider riskier investments, since staying only in low-risk products may grow too slowly and lose purchasing power to inflation and taxes. For a short-term goal of five years or less, risky investments could force you to sell at a loss.
