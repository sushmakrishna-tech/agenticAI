Asset allocation divides investments among assets such as stocks, bonds, and cash. The right mix is personal and changes with your time horizon and risk tolerance. Time horizon is how long you plan to invest toward a goal, and a longer one may make riskier investments more comfortable. Risk tolerance is your ability and willingness to lose some or all of your investment for potentially greater returns.
