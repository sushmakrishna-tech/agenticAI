Volatility describes how sharply prices move up and down. Long-term investors often treat it as background noise, while it can be a real problem for anyone needing the money soon. Diversification is one way to manage it.

## Beta
Beta measures how a stock moves relative to a benchmark, which has a beta of 1. A beta of 1.2 means it has historically moved 120 percent for every 100 percent move in the index, and 0.85 means it has moved less. A stock can have high volatility but low beta if its moves do not track the market.
