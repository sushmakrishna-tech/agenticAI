Asset allocation sets the percentage in each asset class. Diversification spreads investments among and within classes, and rebalancing adjusts regularly to stay on target. Different accounts can have different targets, such as more cash in a down payment fund and more stock in a retirement fund decades away.

## Concentration risk
Allocation alone is not enough. Putting everything into one security or asset class creates concentration risk, which diversification helps reduce.
