Saving and investment products differ in how readily you can get your money, how fast it may grow, and how safe it is. Stocks and bonds both depend on the company staying in business. If a company goes bankrupt and its assets are liquidated, bondholders are paid first, then preferred stockholders. Common stockholders get whatever is left, which may be nothing.
