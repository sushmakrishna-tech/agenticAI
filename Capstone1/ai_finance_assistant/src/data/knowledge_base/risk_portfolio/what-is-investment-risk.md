All investments carry risk. Stocks, bonds, and funds can lose value, even all of it, and insured products such as CDs face inflation risk. Risk types include market, business, political, currency, liquidity, and concentration risk.

## Risk and reward
Higher risk typically goes with higher potential return. FINRA cites long-term average annual returns of just over 10 percent for stocks, around 6 percent for corporate bonds, and 5.5 percent for Treasury bonds. Past returns are not a promise of future results.
