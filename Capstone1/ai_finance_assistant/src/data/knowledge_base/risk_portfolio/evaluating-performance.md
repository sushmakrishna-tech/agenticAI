A yearly review at about the same time each year is often enough to track progress and spot when your allocation has shifted. If you have several accounts, you may need several statements for the full picture.

## Yield and return
Yield is the income an investment pays over a period, usually a year, divided by its price. For a stock, that is the year's dividend divided by the market price, so a stock with no dividend has no yield. Return is all the money you make or lose on an investment.
