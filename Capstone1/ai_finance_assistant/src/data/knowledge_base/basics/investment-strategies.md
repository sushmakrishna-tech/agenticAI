A suitable strategy varies with age, income, assets, risk tolerance, family obligations, and lifestyle. FINRA suggests judging a strategy by whether it fits your goals rather than as simply good or bad. Strategies can be simple, such as a set percentage in diversified stock funds with the rest in bond funds, or complex, such as active strategies with frequent trades or alternative investments.

## Common approaches
Examples include active and passive investing, dollar-cost averaging, market timing, momentum investing, and value investing. A strategy usually changes as circumstances change. A young, single person might accept more risk than someone nearing retirement.
