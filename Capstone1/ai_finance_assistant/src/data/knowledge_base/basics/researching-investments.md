Research is part of an investor's due diligence. Companies and bond issuers must disclose certain information when first selling to the public and periodically afterward. A company that is not registered with the SEC could be a red flag, since scams often involve unregistered companies. Useful resources include EDGAR, 10-K and 8-K reports, mutual fund prospectuses, EMMA for municipal securities and 529 plans, and FINRA's Fund Analyzer for comparing fund costs.
