Monthly cash flow is the difference between your income and your expenses. When income is higher, you have more flexibility to invest.

## Net worth
Net worth is what you own (investments, savings, personal property, and real estate) minus what you owe (such as mortgages, credit card balances, and loans). It helps you set goals, understand your risk tolerance, and estimate how much you might invest. Reviewing it about once a year gives a measurable way to track progress.
