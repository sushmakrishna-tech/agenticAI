Investing carries a greater chance of losing money than saving does. FDIC-insured deposits are protected, but money in securities, mutual funds, and similar investments is not federally insured, even when bought through a bank. You could lose your principal, which is the amount you invested. The tradeoff for that risk is the opportunity to earn more.
