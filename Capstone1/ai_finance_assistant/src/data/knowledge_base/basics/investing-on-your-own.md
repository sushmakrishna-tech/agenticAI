Self-directed investing starts with a financial plan covering how much to invest, for how long, your goals, and your risk tolerance. You are responsible for your own decisions, so research matters. Investor.gov cautions against buying solely on stock tips and suggests confirming that securities are registered using EDGAR. Ways to invest on your own include online investing, direct investing, and dividend reinvestment plans.
