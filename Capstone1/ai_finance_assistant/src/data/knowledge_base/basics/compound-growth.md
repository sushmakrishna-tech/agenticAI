Compounding means earning returns on your original money and on the interest it has already earned. Investor.gov gives this example: saving $365 in a year, the cost of a $1 daily candy bar, and earning 5% a year grows to $465.84 after five years and $1,577.50 after 30 years. Even small amounts can grow substantially given enough time.
