Paying down high-interest debt, such as credit card debt, is one of the best ways to strengthen your finances. The interest saved might exceed the average returns available from most investments. For example, paying off a card charging 20 percent a year saves you that 20 percent. If you cannot clear the debt at once, FINRA describes making a plan of regular, sustainable monthly payments.
