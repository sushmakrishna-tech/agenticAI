A net worth statement lists your assets and liabilities. A negative net worth is not a reason for discouragement, since a financial plan can help turn it positive. The next step is recording what your household earns and spends each month, with a category for saving and investing.

## Paying yourself first
Small everyday expenses can add up. Many people build the habit of saving by paying themselves first, for example by having money automatically deposited from each paycheck into a savings or investment account.
