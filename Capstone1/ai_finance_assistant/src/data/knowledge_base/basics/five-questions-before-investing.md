Before committing money, check whether the seller is licensed, using Investor.gov's free search tool, which also shows disciplinary history and complaints. Next check whether the security is registered with the SEC or exempt, since registration gives investors key information about the company. You can look this up in the SEC's EDGAR database.

## Risk and reward
Also compare the risks with the potential rewards. Greater potential returns come with greater risk of loss, and understanding this tradeoff helps separate legitimate opportunities from scams.
