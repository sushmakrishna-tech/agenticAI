Fees can look small but have a major effect over time. Investor.gov compares a $100,000 investment growing 4% a year for 20 years. With a 0.25% annual fee it reaches about $208,000, with a 0.50% fee about $198,000, and with a 1.00% fee about $179,000.

## Questions to ask about fees
Useful questions include the total cost to buy and sell, how much the investment must rise to break even, what the ongoing account fees are, how the fees compare with similar products, and how an investment professional gets paid.
