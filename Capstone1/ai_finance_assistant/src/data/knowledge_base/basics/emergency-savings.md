Savings are usually kept somewhere safe and accessible, such as savings accounts, checking accounts, and certificates of deposit, which may be insured by the FDIC or NCUA. Many investors keep enough in savings to cover an emergency such as sudden unemployment, and some hold up to six months of income.

## The inflation tradeoff
Safe savings earn low interest. If that interest does not keep up with inflation, the money buys less over time. That is why many people keep some money in savings and invest for longer-term growth.
