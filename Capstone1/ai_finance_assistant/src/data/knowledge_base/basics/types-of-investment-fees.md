Investment costs generally fall into three groups. Transaction costs are charged when you buy or sell, and include commissions, markups or spreads, mutual fund sales loads, and variable annuity surrender charges. Advisory fees pay for advice or portfolio management and may be based on portfolio size. Ongoing expenses recur, such as fund operating expenses and 401(k) administration fees.

## Zero commissions
Some brokerage firms offer zero-commission trading, but that does not mean there are no fees at all.
